# DragonMineZ Adaptive Difficulty System
# Full Development Concept Document
## Personal Difficulty + Team Scaling + Currency Progression System

# 1. System Overview

The Adaptive Difficulty System is a progression-based difficulty framework for DragonMineZ.

The world no longer uses a static difficulty. Every player has their own personal difficulty progression that grows alongside their DragonMineZ progression.

Core features:
- Personal Difficulty
- Calculated Difficulty
- Purchased Difficulty
- Active Difficulty
- Optional Team Scaling
- Lightman's Currency progression
- Configurable in-game settings
- Enemy stat scaling
- Enemy AI progression
- Boss scaling
- Reward scaling

# 2. Core Philosophy

The stronger players become, the stronger the world can become.

Difficulty is always optional and player controlled.

Players spend Lightman's Currency to unlock higher difficulties while also benefiting from teammates when team scaling is enabled.

# 3. Difficulty Values

Every player stores five values:

• Calculated Difficulty
• Purchased Difficulty
• Active Difficulty
• Team Threshold Bonus
• Team Contribution

Calculated Difficulty is based on DragonMineZ progression.

Prestige 0:
Calculated Difficulty = DMZ Level

Prestige 1+:
Calculated Difficulty = DMZ Level × (Prestige × Prestige Multiplier)

Default Prestige Multiplier = 10

Purchased Difficulty is bought using Lightman's Currency and is stored separately from Calculated Difficulty.

Personal Maximum Difficulty:

Calculated Difficulty + Purchased Difficulty

Active Difficulty is the difficulty currently selected by the player and can never exceed the player's current maximum.

# 4. Team Scaling

Difficulty is NOT shared globally.

Every player has their own personal difficulty.

Players may optionally enable Team Scaling.

Each online teammate increases that player's maximum difficulty threshold by 10%.

Example:

Calculated Maximum: 500
3 teammates

Maximum becomes:

500 × 1.30 = 650

A player with 10,000 calculated maximum becomes:

10,000 × 1.30 = 13,000

In addition to the threshold increase, teammates may contribute their available difficulty.

Example:

Player A:
Maximum = 500

Player B:
Maximum = 15,000

If Team Scaling is enabled, Player A may temporarily access higher difficulty based on Player B's contribution.

This borrowed difficulty is temporary and disappears when teammates leave.

Players can choose between:

• Personal Only
• Threshold Bonus Only
• Full Team Scaling

# 6. Purchasing Difficulty

Players spend Lightman's Currency to permanently unlock Purchased Difficulty.

Example upgrades:

+100
+1,000
+10,000

Cost formula is configurable.

Default:

Cost = Base Cost × (Purchased Difficulty / Cost Scaling)

Lowering difficulty is always free.

# 7. Administrator Configuration

Everything should be configurable in-game.

Permission:
difficulty.admin

Command:
/difficulty admin

Editable settings include:

- Prestige multiplier
- Level multiplier
- Team bonus %
- Contribution %
- Base cost
- Cost scaling
- Reward scaling
- Health scaling
- Damage scaling
- Defense scaling
- Ability unlock tiers

Example commands:

/difficulty settings
/difficulty set prestigeMultiplier 10
/difficulty set teamBonus 10
/difficulty set baseCost 100000
/difficulty set costScaling 1000
/difficulty set rewardScaling 1000
/difficulty reload

# 8. Difficulty States

Green = Below progression
Yellow = Balanced
Orange = Personal Maximum
Purple = Team Boosted
Red = Extreme

# 9. Mob Scaling

Scaling is calculated once when a mob spawns.

Default scaling:

Health +1% per Difficulty
Damage +1% per Difficulty
Defense +3% per Difficulty
Movement +0.1% every 100 Difficulty

DragonMineZ mobs additionally gain:

Health +1%
Physical Damage +1%
Defense +3%
Ki Damage +1%

# 16. Boss Scaling

Bosses inherit player difficulty.

- Damage
- Defense

# 17. Performance Requirements

Never calculate every tick.

Cache:

- Player difficulty
- Team bonus
- Team contribution
- Mob scaling

Update only when:

- Player joins
- Player leaves
- Level changes
- Prestige changes
- Difficulty changes
- Team changes

# Final Vision

Players do not simply become stronger than the world.

They choose how dangerous the world becomes.

Low-level players benefit from experienced teammates while experienced players gain even greater challenge ceilings.

Difficulty becomes an endless progression system that keeps DragonMineZ challenging throughout the entire game.
